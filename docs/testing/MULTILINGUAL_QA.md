## 1. Native-Speaker Review Guidelines
Before recording voice assets or deploying in live municipal pilots:
1. **Kannada Reviewer:** Ensure terms like *"ದುಡಿಯುವ ಬಂಡವಾಳ ಸಾಲ"* (working capital loan) and *"ವ್ಯಾಪಾರ ಪ್ರಮಾಣಪತ್ರ"* (CoV) are natural for vendor communities in Bengaluru/Mysuru.
2. **Hindi Reviewer:** Ensure clarity of *"कार्यशील पूंजी ऋण"* and that the 7% interest subsidy is clearly understood as an incentive on timely repayment, not a loan waiver.
3. **Tamil Reviewer:** Confirm that *"நடைமுறை மூலதனக் கடன்"* and *"வியாபாரச் சான்றிதழ்"* match everyday vendor vocabulary in Chennai/Madurai.
4. **Bengali Reviewer:** Confirm that *"চলতি মূলধন ঋণ"* and Section 18(3) thirty-day notice provisions are phrased naturally for vendors in Kolkata/Howrah.
