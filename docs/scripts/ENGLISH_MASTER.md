### Script 2 – PM SVANidhi Scheme Explanation
"Based on the information you shared, you may be eligible to apply for a PM SVANidhi working-capital loan.

This scheme provides collateral-free loans to eligible street vendors, subject to verification and lender approval.

The scheme offers loans in three progressive stages. You may receive a first loan of up to ₹15,000. After successfully repaying the first loan and meeting the applicable requirements, you may be considered for a second loan of up to ₹25,000. Similarly, after successfully repaying the second loan, you may be considered for a third loan of up to ₹50,000.

If you repay your loan on time, you may be eligible for an interest subsidy of 7% per year, subject to the scheme rules. This subsidy helps reduce your interest burden. It is not a loan waiver or a free grant.

For application and eligibility verification, a valid Certificate of Vending or Letter of Recommendation may be used, subject to the applicable scheme requirements. The lending institution will also assess your application.

For complete eligibility information, please consult your local Urban Local Body or the official PM SVANidhi portal.

Would you like to check your eligibility now?"

### Script 4 – Audio Rights Card
"Here is your Kavach Vaani Audio Rights Card.

Part 1 – Scheme Information:
You do not need to pay commission to intermediaries to apply for PM SVANidhi. Do not pay bribes or unauthorized charges to brokers.

Part 2 – Statutory Rights Under the Street Vendors Act, 2014:
Under Section 18(3) of the Street Vendors Act, 2014, a street vendor cannot be relocated or evicted by the local authority from the place specified in their Certificate of Vending unless they have been given thirty days' notice in the manner prescribed by the scheme.

This protection applies specifically to the location mentioned in the Certificate of Vending. It does not provide general immunity from other applicable laws or lawful enforcement actions.

Part 3 – Official Support Channels:
For official assistance and verified information about your PM SVANidhi application, please use the official PM SVANidhi portal or contact your Urban Local Body."
