# 🎬 3-MINUTE YOUTUBE DEMO SCRIPT
### Product: Kavach Vaani (कवच वाणी)

**Total Duration:** 2 minutes 45 seconds  
**Visual Style:** Screen recording of the mobile interface with live microphone voice input.

---

### Segment 1: The Problem (0:00 - 0:35)
- **Speaker:**  
  *"Over 450 million informal workers in India—street vendors, auto drivers, construction laborers—are cut off from government welfare schemes like PM SVANidhi, e-Shram, and PM-JAY. Why? Because existing government portals require reading bureaucratic English or Hindi, typing multi-page forms, and overcoming CAPTCHAs. As a result, poor workers lose up to ₹3,000 to corrupt middlemen just to file a simple form.*  
  *Today, we are presenting Kavach Vaani (कवच वाणी)—an empathetic, voice-native welfare access companion designed for Bharat."*

---

### Segment 2: One-Touch Voice Entry & Scheme Matching (0:35 - 1:15)
- **Action on Screen:**  
  1. Open `http://localhost:3000`. Show the clean, high-contrast mobile screen with the glowing orange microphone.  
  2. Select Hindi or Kannada.  
  3. Tap the mic button and speak:  
     *"Main Lucknow mein chai ka thela lagata hoon, naya burner khareedne ke liye 15,000 rupaye ka loan chahiye."*  
  4. The soundwave bars animate in real time.  
- **Speaker:**  
  *"Notice how the worker doesn't type a single character. Kavach Vaani instantly extracts the occupation—Street Vendor, location—Lucknow, and the need—₹15,000 working capital loan. It queries the statutory scheme engine and speaks back in warm vernacular Hindi..."*  
- **Audio Output from App:**  
  *"Aap PM SVANidhi yojana ke patra hain! Isme bina kisi guarantee ke 7% byaj subsidy par loan milta hai. Kya main aapka form abhi bhar doon?"*

---

### Segment 3: Guided Voice Form Filling (1:15 - 1:55)
- **Action on Screen:**  
  1. Click 'Fill This Application By Voice' or say 'Haan'.  
  2. The Live Application Card appears.  
  3. Speak answers one by one (Name, Mobile, Aadhaar digits, Bank/UPI).  
  4. Show how the visual card populates each field in real time with green confirmation checkmarks.  
- **Speaker:**  
  *"Instead of showing overwhelming form fields, Kavach Vaani acts like a caring companion, asking simple questions one by one. Sensitive details like Aadhaar and UPI numbers are repeated aloud for voice confirmation."*

---

### Segment 4: The Audio Rights Card & Anti-Extortion Safeguard (1:55 - 2:35)
- **Action on Screen:**  
  1. Say 'Submit maadi' or 'Haan'. Confetti explodes on screen!  
  2. The **Audio Rights Card** appears with official reference number.  
  3. Click 'Listen to Rights' to play the voice note.  
  4. Click 'Download Official PDF Card' and show the generated clean PDF receipt.  
- **Speaker:**  
  *"This is the breakthrough: The Audio Rights Card. Most informal workers fear police eviction or municipal extortion. Kavach Vaani generates a spoken legal rights note citing Section 3 of the Street Vendors Act 2014, warning that no officer can evict them without a 30-day notice, and provides official anti-corruption grievance helplines."*

---

### Segment 5: Hackathon Compliance & Closing (2:35 - 2:45)
- **Speaker:**  
  *"Kavach Vaani starts with a single docker-compose command, requires zero secret keys to run, and is 100% open-source on GitHub. Thank you!"*
